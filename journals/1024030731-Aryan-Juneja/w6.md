# Week 6 — Reminder Data Structure and Deadline Management

## Objective

The objective of Week 6 was to plan the database structure required for storing examination reminder information and supporting deadline management in GovExam Alert.

## Work Completed

During this week, I studied the data requirements for managing examination deadlines and user reminders.

The database structure was planned to support the association between examination records, important dates and user-related reminder information. The design also considered how reminder data could be retrieved efficiently by the backend.

## Technical Work

- Identified data requirements for examination reminders.
- Planned the reminder-related database structure.
- Reviewed relationships between users, examinations and reminders.
- Identified important date fields required for deadline management.
- Considered primary keys and foreign keys for related records.
- Planned database integration with backend reminder operations.

## Project Understanding

The database will support reminder functionality by maintaining relevant examination dates and, if required by the final design, user-specific reminder records. These records will help the backend retrieve the information needed to display upcoming deadlines.

## GitHub Work

The Week 6 database planning and schema-related work was documented as part of the team's ongoing development process in GitHub.

## Outcome

The initial database requirements for reminder information and deadline management were identified, providing a foundation for future implementation and integration.
