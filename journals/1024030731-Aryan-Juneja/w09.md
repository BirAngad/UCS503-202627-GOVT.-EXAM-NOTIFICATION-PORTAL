# Week 9 — Database Validation and Data Consistency

## Objective
The objective of this week was to review database requirements and understand how data validation and consistency could be maintained in the GovExam Alert system.

## Work Completed
- Reviewed the examination data structure.
- Identified the importance of maintaining unique examination records.
- Studied how invalid or incomplete data could affect the application.
- Reviewed the importance of primary keys and foreign keys.
- Considered validation requirements for examination information.
- Studied how duplicate and inconsistent records could be avoided.

## Technical Work
The database activities focused on:
- Primary key identification.
- Foreign key relationships.
- Data validation requirements.
- Consistency of examination records.
- Handling missing or invalid values.
- Database integrity concepts.

## Learning and Understanding
I understood the importance of database integrity and how primary keys and foreign keys help maintain relationships between different records. I also learned why data validation is necessary for reliable application functionality.

## Outcome
Database validation requirements and data consistency considerations were reviewed. Further implementation and testing were required to ensure reliable data management.
